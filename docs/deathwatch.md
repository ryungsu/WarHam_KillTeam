# Deathwatch local development support

Source: retained data-original/upstream.json (kt24_v4 community snapshot, imported 2026-10-14), IMP-DW.
Official composition cross-check: https://assets.warhammer-community.com/eng_29-10_kill_team_team_rules_deathwatch-n9uexmycj0-mnutiohz2j.pdf

11 operative types, 5 per team, no repeated operative, at most 1 GRAVIS. Watch Sergeant is optional; Adaptable Armoury grants one extra equipment selection. Weapons are fixed per operative; weapon profiles are displayed together.

The linked October official PDF is an older document: its ammunition options, Veteran Astartes and several ploys differ from the retained JSON. Do not overwrite the community snapshot with those older rules or claim latest balance verification. Korean text translates the retained JSON, with English retained for inspection. Operative portraits are extracted from the linked official Deathwatch datacards.

Included in the roster-home / Deathwatch release.
